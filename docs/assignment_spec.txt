Practical Assignment  
 
Learning Goals 
 
1.​ Gain a deep understanding of an advanced topic in ML by means of a literature 
review. 
2.​ Understand the methodology for development of innovative solutions for advanced 
ML topics. 
 
Description​
 
In this project the students will develop a research project on an advanced ML topic of their 
choice. The goal is understand how reliable, innovative solutions should be developed for 
advanced ML problems. Given a paper of their choice, the students will investigate a 
limitation of the corresponding work. This involves a short but systematic literature review 
on the topic and an empirical study concerning the contribution in the selected paper. Based 
on this, students will propose an alternative solution and then empirically compare it to the 
original approach, to assess if the limitations identified have been addressed. The results of 
the project will be discussed with the course lecturers. 
 
Workplan 
 
Phase 1 – paper selection 
-​
Select a paper on an advanced ML topic, in collaboration with the course lecturers. 
-​
The paper should include code, to minimize the implementation effort. 
-​
Students are encouraged to select papers from topics addressed in the 
course, but this is not mandatory. 
-​
Students are encouraged to select recent papers from important conferences 
and journals in ML (e.g., NeurIPS, KDD, ICML, ICLR, ECMLPKDD, Discovery 
Science, Intelligent Data Analysis, Machine Learning journal, Journal of 
Machine Learning Research, Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery journal, 
International Journal of Data Science and Analytics).​
 
Phase 2 – analysis of selected contribution 
-​
Analyze the contribution of the selected paper to identify an interesting and relevant 
limitation. The analysis method should be discussed with the course lecturers but it 
usually involves:

-​
Empirical evaluation of the contribution of the paper on a set of benchmark 
problems.  
-​
Literature review, focused on the limitation of interest.  
-​
Given the limited time for the project, this analysis may have to be simplified.  
-​
Depending on the project, other methods may be more suitable.  
 
Phase 3 – development of an alternative approach 
-​
Propose a variant that is expected to address the limitation identified. 
-​
Implement the proposed variant. 
-​
Empirically evaluate if the proposed variant adequately addresses the limitation 
identified, using the same set of benchmark problems used to evaluate the original 
contribution.  
 
Evaluation moments 
 
-​
Checkpoint 1: Discussion of paper selected for project. This consists of a 3min 
presentation followed by a 5min discussion with the course lecturers.  
-​
Checkpoint 2: Discussion of problem addressed and planned solution. This consists 
of a 5min presentation followed by a 10min discussion with the course lecturers.  
-​
Project submission: Your assignment should be submitted in Moodle with a 
compressed file containing the following items: 
-​
the source of a ready-to-execute notebook with all the code necessary to run 
to obtain the presented results, including any complementary files needed to 
execute your notebook (e.g. data files, data objects); 
-​
slides for presentation (PDF format) focusing on the main issues of the 
assignment; any additional information that cannot be presented in that time 
slot can be included as annexes to the presentation; the total number of 
slides in the final submission, including annexes, should not exceed 40. 
-​
Final project discussion: This consists of an 8min presentation followed by a 12min 
discussion with the course lecturers.  
 
All checkpoints are mandatory. Any missing evaluation moment will be graded with 0. 
The specific dates of these moments are indicated in the course schedule. 
 
Groups 
 
The practical assignment is mandatory and should be performed by groups of three 
students. Please select your group in Moodle until the deadline indicated in the course 
schedule.
